Groq, Inc. is an American technology company specializing in high-performance artificial intelligence hardware and software, specifically optimized for **AI inference** (the process of running a pre-trained AI model).

Here are the key points to understand about Groq:

*   **Language Processing Unit (LPU):** Groq is best known for developing a custom processor called the **LPU (Language Processing Unit)**. Unlike traditional GPUs (like those from Nvidia), which are designed for general-purpose parallel computing and training, the LPU is an ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) built specifically to handle the demands of large language models (LLMs).
*   **Speed and Determinism:** The LPU architecture is designed for extremely low-latency, high-speed performance. It uses a "deterministic" design, meaning its hardware operation is controlled by a compiler rather than reactive systems like caches or branch predictors. This allows for highly predictable and incredibly fast generation of text in AI applications.
*   **Founding:** The company was founded in 2016 by a team of engineers, including Jonathan Ross, who was a key contributor to the development of Google’s Tensor Processing Unit (TPU).
*   **GroqCloud:** Groq provides a platform called **GroqCloud™**, which allows developers to use APIs to run open-source large language models (such as Llama or Mixtral) on Groq’s infrastructure, experiencing the speed of their LPU technology.
*   **Industry Position:** Groq is considered a significant player in the AI hardware space, focusing on solving the bottleneck of "AI inference speed" to make real-time, interactive AI applications more viable.
