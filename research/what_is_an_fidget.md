The term "fidget" can refer to a few different things depending on the context:

*   **Fidgeting:** This refers to the act of making small, restless movements, usually with your hands or feet. People often fidget when they are nervous, bored, impatient, or feeling energetic and unable to sit still.
*   **Fidget Toy:** This is a popular category of small devices or objects designed to be manipulated in the hand. They are often used as stress-relief tools or to help improve focus by providing a sensory outlet for restless energy. Common examples include fidget spinners, fidget cubes, and "pop-it" toys.
*   **Other uses:** In niche contexts, "Fidget" can also refer to a subgenre of electronic music called "Fidget House," or it can be a proper name, such as the historical British Admiralty tugboat named *ST Fidget*.
