The term "fidget" can refer to a few different things depending on the context:

*   Fidgeting (Action): This refers to the act of making small, restless, or repetitive movements. People often fidget when they are nervous, bored, impatient, or struggling to sit still. It is a common behavior that can sometimes help people regulate their attention or energy levels.
*   Fidget Toy (Object): This is a category of small objects designed to be manipulated by hand. These toys are often used to help people manage stress, anxiety, or focus. Popular examples include fidget spinners, fidget cubes, pop-its, and stress balls.
*   Other contexts:
    *   Fidget house: A subgenre of electronic house music.
    *   ST Fidget: A historic British Admiralty tugboat.
